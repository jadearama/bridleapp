Parawing bridle Calc 1 · A-row stepper

amain → a3 from amain → a2, given the arc
How this works · tap to expand
  1. You measure (or pick) the bridle length from amain → a2.
  2. You decide where a3 sits — i.e. the arc-step s from a2 along the leading edge.
  3. You define the leading-edge arc with its radius R and total LE length. Together they fix the wing's curvature and span.
  4. The calc places amain on the wing's vertical centreline at whatever height makes amain → a2 = your input. Then it computes amain → a3.
Assumptions (v1): 2D front view; wing leading edge is a circular arc; amain sits centred on the wing's symmetry line; geometry is symmetric. No fore/aft (chord) component yet — every a-row attachment is treated as on the leading edge. Tell me in chat what to extend.
Math (for the record):
θ₂ = d_a2 / R   h = √(L_a2² − R²·sin²θ₂) − R·(1 − cosθ₂)
θ₃ = (d_a2 + s) / R   L_a3 = √(R²·sin²θ₃ + (R + h − R·cosθ₃)²)
